Trial Run 1: Sensor Readings Service

Time limit: 3 hours. Build it, test it, deploy it to DigitalOcean App Platform.

The brief

We operate a fleet of field devices that report temperature. Each device sends readings to us over HTTP, one at a time, and sometimes in bursts after being offline. Devices are not perfectly reliable: they may send the same reading more than once, send garbage values when a sensor fails, or have a clock that is wrong.

We need a REST service that accepts these readings, keeps them, and lets an operator ask about a device: what has it reported, and how has it been behaving. The service must be running at a public URL on DigitalOcean by the end of the session.

A reading sent by a device looks like this:

{
  "reading_id": "a1b2c3",                 // unique id chosen by the device
  "device_id": "device-42",
  "timestamp": "2026-09-20T14:03:00Z",
  "temperature_c": 21.5
}

The endpoints, the validation rules, the storage, and the scope are yours to decide. Tell us what you chose and why.

How you will be judged

  • Does it work at the public URL?
  • Is the code organized, or is it one giant file?
  • Are errors clear and are status codes correct?
  • Do the tests actually prove something?
  • Does it behave like something you'd run for real: logs, settings, a health check?
  • Can you explain every choice, including what you skipped and why?

Your first 15 minutes

Before writing code, derive your own list from the brief: nouns are resources, verbs are writes, questions the operator asks are reads. Write down validation rules by asking what a nonsense value looks like for each field. Rank everything as must, should, or nice. Put it in spec.md. Then open the section below and compare.
